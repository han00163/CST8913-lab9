# Cost Optimization Strategy 



## Summary: 

The Azure cost can be made significantly lower with optimization in six areas:

1. **Commitment** to steady workloads(reserved instances/reserved capacity).
2. **Scale** variable workloads(auto-scaling, right-sizing). 
3. **Schedule** idle workloads(pause/scale to zero).
4. **Use spare capacity** for interruptible jobs(Spot VMs).
5. **Simplify the architecture** to avoid duplicate services and unnecessary replicas. 
6. **Govern** spending continuously(tags, budgets, Advisor, Policy).
